Nike · Pegasus 42, Women's

More Return.

The Pegasus 42's real upgrade is energy return — 15% more than the Pegasus 41. This campaign turns that engineering fact into an emotional one: every mile gives back more than it costs, matching runners to a real colorway based on what they need from today's run.
