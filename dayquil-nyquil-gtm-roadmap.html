Vicks DayQuil / NyQuil Honey · GTM Roadmap

Honey, You're Covered.

A three-phase go-to-market plan for the Honey line extension, sequencing trade, retail, and consumer touchpoints around the shelf-decision moment.

Weeks 1–3
Pre-Launch
Trade Sell-in to pharmacy and grocery buyers with shelf-share data on the "tastes medicinal" objection, securing end-cap commitments ahead of cold season.
Retail Finalize shelf-talker and end-cap creative production; confirm placement adjacent to existing DayQuil/NyQuil core line, not as a separate SKU shoppers have to hunt for.
Consumer Seed sampling through pharmacist partnerships and cold-and-flu season checklists distributed via retail partners.
Weeks 4–6
Launch Week
Retail End-cap and shelf-talker go live nationally, timed to the start of peak cold-and-flu season for maximum category traffic.
Consumer Paid social and search targeting people actively searching "cold medicine that doesn't taste bad" and related taste-objection queries.
Consumer Sampling continues in pharmacies with a small honey-drizzle visual cue on packaging to differentiate from the core line at a glance.
Weeks 7–12
Sustain
Trade Share early sell-through data with retail partners to defend and expand shelf space for the remainder of the season.
Consumer Shift paid media toward retargeting past core-line buyers with a "you haven't tried Honey yet" message.
Retail Rotate shelf-talker messaging seasonally (flu peak vs. tail-end) to keep the display feeling current through the full season.
