Why I do this

Honestly, what pulls me into marketing is the psychology of it. Why does one shade name make you reach for the blush and not another? Why does a stat about a shoe's energy return make you think about your own? I like poking at the small, half-conscious reasons people buy things, then building the real campaign around it instead of a made-up one.

This site walks through campaigns across beauty, footwear, sleep, toys, sports, music streaming, social platforms, consumer health, and consumer tech, each with different deliverables depending on what the idea actually needed: an interactive quiz, a social series, a video storyboard, an OOH concept, a working UI mockup, a GTM launch roadmap. Same instinct every time: start with something the brand already had and hadn't said out loud yet.
