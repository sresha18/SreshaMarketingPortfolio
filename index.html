Why I do this

Honestly, what pulls me into marketing is the psychology of it. Why does one shade name make you reach for the blush and not another? Why does a stat about a shoe's energy return make you think about your own? I like poking at the small, half-conscious reasons people buy things, then building the real campaign around it instead of a made-up one.

This site walks through 14 campaigns across beauty, footwear, sleep, toys, sports, music, social platforms, health and tech. Each has deliverables matched to what the idea needed: a quiz, a social series, a storyboard, a UI mockup, a launch roadmap. Same instinct every time: start with something the brand already had and hadn't said out loud yet.
